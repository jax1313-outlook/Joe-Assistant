## 9. Learning Matrix Summary

Agents may learn from approved Library assets, Mike-approved final outputs, accepted and rejected drafts, decision logs, walkthrough reports, final archived packets, approved templates, approved rate sheets, sealed compliance records, completed requirement matrices, and submitted package records.

Agents may not treat Research Scout reports, public examples, unverified templates, AI-generated drafts, unapproved notes, old packets, expired forms, third-party samples, or internet content as truth.

## 11. Conflict Notice Standard

Use a Conflict Notice when a task would require:

- inventing facts
- claiming unsupported eligibility
- signing or certifying
- submitting an application
- approving a package
- deciding rates
- deciding compliance
- deciding legal sufficiency
- changing doctrine
- changing architecture
- changing role boundaries
- promoting a draft into Library truth
- blurring Library and Archive
- treating research as truth
- exposing internal systems publicly without approved authentication
- creating new agents
- creating new workflows without approval
- bypassing Manager
- bypassing Mike

## 12. Government Packet Production Directive

Government packet material is not Research Scout work.

Library stores approved reusable assets.

Archive stores completed package history.

## 14. Semantic Review Rule

Semantic Review > Textual Review.

Do not ask only whether something works, merges, reads cleanly, or looks complete.

Ask what it means, what role it affects, what doctrine it touches, what assumptions it changes, what dependency it creates, what hidden burden it introduces, and what future drift it could cause.

## 15. Website / Presentation Layer Direction

Website/presentation work is a future presentation-layer effort, not a replacement for Dispatch.

```text

```
