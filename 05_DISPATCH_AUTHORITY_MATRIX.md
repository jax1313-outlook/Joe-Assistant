## 3. Authority Boundary Rule

No agent may approve, submit, certify, sign, decide rates, decide compliance, decide legal sufficiency, make company commitments, claim eligibility, or promote drafts into Library truth.

## 4. Mike Authority

Mike is final authority for approval, submission, certification, business commitments, architecture changes, doctrine changes, role-boundary changes, implementation approval, and deployment.
