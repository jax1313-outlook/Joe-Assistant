# Mission Visibility

Mission visibility means every party who needs to know the state of a mission
can see that state without asking a person for it.

## Why it matters

A driver who has to phone in for status is doing dispatch work while driving.
Visibility removes that call. The mission carries its own status.

## What is visible

- Current leg and next stop.
- Appointment windows, and whether they are confirmed.
- Whether a decision is required, and from whom.
- Any change since the driver last looked.

## What visibility does not do

Visibility reports state. It does not approve, dispatch, or decide. A visible
mission still waits on the driver for every operational decision.
