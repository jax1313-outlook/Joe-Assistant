# Driver First

Driver first means the driver's time and attention are the scarcest resource in
the operation, and systems are built to spend less of both.

## Practical tests

1. Does this require the driver to look at a screen while moving? If yes, it
   fails.
2. Does this ask the driver a question the system could answer itself?
3. Does this produce a document nobody asked for?

## Answer first

Answer the question, then offer the detail. A driver asking about tomorrow
wants what changed, why it matters, and whether a decision is required.
