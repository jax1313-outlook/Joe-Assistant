# Rate Floor Policy

A rate floor is the lowest rate per mile a lane is worth running, after fuel,
deadhead, and the time the lane actually costs.

## Using a floor

- A rate under the floor is a pass or a counter, not a negotiation to win.
- A rate at the floor is a decision, not an automatic yes.
- The floor is recorded per lane and reviewed when fuel moves materially.

## What a floor is not

A floor is not a target. Running at the floor every week is a slow loss.
